Elevor360D Vehicle Maintenance Tracker Free V1
Upload the files and folders to a dedicated HTTPS website location.
Free app with local vehicle and expense records, due-date overview, backup/restore and rotating advertising.
Advertising feed: advertising/adverts.json. Configure a central feed in ad-config.json if required.
Dates are displayed when opening the app; background notifications are not provided.

V1.2: Default advert appears immediately, even if the feed cannot load. A valid feed overrides it.
